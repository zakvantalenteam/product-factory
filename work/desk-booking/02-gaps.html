Gaps - DeskBook

25 gaps: 0 blockers, 8 high, 12 medium, 5 low. Nothing stops the build from starting, but the 8 HIGH ones would make us build the wrong thing if we guess. Answers you already gave (intake 1-5 and Q1-Q11) are built in and not repeated here. Two of them clash with each other: see G1.

Most important (12)

G1 - HIGH - Can colleagues see each other's desks? Two earlier answers disagree. Intake question 5 says "own team only (manager and peers)". Q4 says "own bookings only; manager sees direct reports". Example: Asha and Ravi report to Meera. Can Asha see that Ravi sits at BLR-3-14 on Tuesday? Recommended: follow the stricter Q4. Only managers see their direct reports; peers see nothing of each other. Easy to open up later, hard to take back. If not decided: the spec follows Q4; if you meant peers too, the team view is rebuilt.

G2 - HIGH - Who sees a named person's no-shows? Q4 says reports show counts, not names. Q7 says the no-show rate is visible to the manager and admin. Section 7 of the PRD: "what happens with no-shows repeatedly?". Example: Meera wants to know that Ravi missed 3 desks this month. The admin report must not name him. In Germany this kind of personal attendance data may also need the works council's OK. Recommended: manager sees each direct report's count (last 30 days) in the team view; admin sees counts per office only, never names. Ask Legal and HR to confirm before go-live in Germany and France. If not decided: either names leak into reports or managers get no no-show information.

G3 - HIGH - A desk, floor or whole office is blocked while people have bookings on it. PRD 4.1: "Admins can block a desk for maintenance." Example: BLR-3-14 is blocked 3-5 Nov, and Asha and Ravi already hold it for 4 Nov. Recommended: the admin sees "2 bookings will be cancelled" and must confirm; both people are emailed and told to book another desk. Blocks cannot start in the past. Same rule for retiring a floor, closing an office or adding a closed day. If not decided: people arrive and find their desk gone, or admins cannot block a desk that is in use.

G4 - HIGH - Morning desk and afternoon desk on the same day? PRD 4.2: "One booking per employee per day" and "half day (morning/afternoon)". Example: Asha wants desk A in the morning and a quiet room desk B in the afternoon. One booking or two? Recommended: one booking per day, and it is full, morning or afternoon. Two different desks on one day are not allowed in release 1. If not decided: the spec uses one per day; allowing two later changes the rule everywhere.

G5 - HIGH - Group booking: all or nothing, and how big? PRD 4.4: "book a group of desks near each other for a team day" (zones answered in Q8). Example: Meera picks 5 people. The zone has only 4 free desks, and Ravi already has a desk. Recommended: either every selected person gets a desk in one zone or nobody does. People who already have a desk are skipped and shown first. Largest group: 20. If not decided: some people get a desk, some do not, and the team day fails halfway.

G6 - HIGH - What does "2 weeks ahead" mean? PRD 4.2: "up to 2 weeks ahead". Weekends and public holidays are not mentioned. Example: Today is Monday 3 Nov. Can Asha book Monday 17 Nov? Can she book a public holiday? Recommended: today up to 14 days ahead, working days only; each office keeps its own working days and closed days. The 14 is a company setting the admin can change. If not decided: people book closed days, or lose a day of the horizon.

G7 - HIGH - Who counts as a manager, and what about people who change manager or leave? PRD 3: "Manager - sees team attendance". Q11 covers the data but not these cases. Example: Ravi changes manager on Wednesday; Nisha leaves on Friday with bookings for next week. Recommended: a manager is anyone SuccessFactors lists as manager of at least one active person; no separate setup. When a person changes manager the team view moves with the next nightly copy and bookings stay. When someone leaves, her future bookings are cancelled and the desks freed, without an email to her. If not decided: manager lists need manual upkeep, or desks stay held by people who have left.

G8 - HIGH - How long do bookings with names stay? Q10 covers the change history (2 years) only. Bookings show who sat where. Example: Asha left in 2027. Her 2026 bookings are still in the database in 2030. Recommended: bookings keep the person's name for 2 years, then are made anonymous; counts in reports stay. If not decided: Legal (especially Germany and France) can block release, and the data grows without limit.

G9 - MEDIUM - Check-in rules. PRD 4.3: "button in the app, or QR code on the desk". Example: Asha scans the neighbour desk by mistake. Or checks in from her sofa at 06:30. Recommended: check-in opens 06:00 office time (12:00 for afternoon bookings) and closes at release. A scan of a desk that is not hers does not check her in; if that desk is free she can move her booking there. No location check. If not decided: people check in for desks they do not use, or scanning the wrong desk gives an error.

G10 - MEDIUM - What do "capacity", "no-show" and half days mean in reports? PRD 4.5: "booked vs checked-in vs capacity, no-show rate". Example: A floor has 50 desks, 2 are blocked, 10 bookings are half days. Recommended: capacity = bookable desks only (blocked and retired do not count); a half day counts as half a desk-day; no-show rate = released bookings divided by bookings not cancelled before the release time. If not decided: Facilities and managers read different percentages for the same day.

G11 - MEDIUM - Desks reserved for people who need them (accessibility). Not in the PRD; the review raised it. Example: Ravi needs an ergonomic desk. Who decides he may book it? Recommended: admin flags desks as reserved and keeps a list of people allowed to book them. The list holds names only, never a reason. No approval workflow in release 1. If not decided: everyone books these desks, or we store health information.

G12 - MEDIUM - Which messages exactly? PRD 4.6: "Reminder the evening before, and a message when a booking is released. Etc." Example: What does Ravi get when Meera books him a desk? Recommended: email only, five messages: evening reminder (18:00 office time, working days only), booking released, booked or cancelled for you by your manager, cancelled because the desk was blocked, no-show reminder after 3 in a month. English only for now. If not decided: we build only the two named messages and people miss the rest.

Smaller points

  • G13 - MEDIUM - "Works on mobile" (PRD 5): app or browser? Recommended: responsive web in the phone browser from 360 px wide, no native app; the QR code opens the browser.
  • G14 - MEDIUM - Cancel "any time" (4.2) after check-in? Recommended: cancel until check-in; afterwards "Release desk" frees it for the rest of the day.
  • G15 - MEDIUM - Asha arrives at 10:05 after release (4.3): what does she see? Recommended: free desks first, one line saying her booking was released; she may book another free desk if the cut-off has not passed.
  • G16 - MEDIUM - Booking for today: until when? Recommended: until 10:00 office time for full day and morning, until 14:00 for afternoon.
  • G17 - MEDIUM - Who administers: one global team or one per office (4.1)? Recommended: one global admin team now; per-office admins later.
  • G18 - MEDIUM - A new hire signs in before the nightly copy has her. Recommended: "Your profile is not ready yet, try tomorrow"; no booking until she is in the copy.
  • G19 - LOW - Can an employee book in an office that is not her own (4.2)? Recommended: yes.
  • G20 - LOW - Excel export content and size (4.5). Recommended: what is on screen, up to 100,000 rows, counts only, no names.
  • G21 - LOW - German and French "later" (5): which release? Recommended: release 2; all texts translatable from day one.
  • G22 - LOW - No free desk: is there a waiting list? Not in the PRD. Recommended: none in release 1; show other days and offices that have desks.
  • G23 - LOW - How is "90 % get a desk" (Q1) counted? Recommended: share of searches that found at least one free desk, per office, reported weekly.
  • G24 - MEDIUM - Creating 1,500 desks one by one (4.1) is slow. Recommended: CSV import of desks (code, floor, zone, features) in release 1.
  • G25 - MEDIUM - (added after GP, option A) What is "your usual desk"? The prototype offers it first on Today and in Plan my days, but nothing defines it. Example: Asha booked PUN-3-14 nine times and PUN-3-15 three times last month. Recommended (same as Q97): the desk she booked most often in the last 60 days, with at least 3 bookings; a tie goes to the most recent; none otherwise (then the next reason applies). Nothing to set by hand. If not decided: developers guess, and people distrust the proposal.
