# sdlc skill: 8. Stages

Part of the `sdlc` skill (SKILL.md next to this file), read when its step comes. Read it when each stage starts.

## 8. Stages

| # | Stage | Who | Output in `<work>/<id>/` | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spec (when on the team) | `develop`: `spec-analyst` (mode `dor`, then `task`: the technical part) or `performance-engineer`; `fix`: `bug-investigator` | `brief.md`, `spec.md` (bugs: also `fix-proof.md` with the reproduction, the proven root cause, the regression test and ring; speed: `perf.md`) | `handoff_check.py` passes (`--fix` for a fix); `plan <id>` |
| 2 | Design (when on the team; `develop`, and `fix` only when the fix plan needs a card or crosses layers) | `review_pack.py <id> --design`, then `solution-architect` and the design experts on the team, all in one message, all reading `design-pack.md` (you merge their notes into `design.md`); `qa-engineer` (`characterize`) at risk medium or high; `qa-lead` (`plan`) in the same message; `ux-designer` builds the prototype for UI and you ask for the pick | `design.md` (with `## Planned files`), `test-plan.md`, `decisions/*.md`, `ui.md` / `perf.md` / `sf.md` / `ai.md`, `safety.md` `## Before build` | Every criterion maps to a planned change; `handoff_check.py` passes |
| 3 | Build | `develop` and `fix`: the area agents from your build briefs (section 4), in parallel where their files do not overlap, after the "before" steps of section 5; you integrate their results and run the "after" steps | The code change | Follows AGENTS.md, `.claude/rules/`, the repo's area skills and the planned files; the "after" steps of section 5 pass |
| 4 | Review | `develop` and `fix`: `review_pack.py <id>`, then one `code-reviewer` run (`focus: all`, re-checks its own findings; for a fix the delta pack of the changed lines); an expert review pass (ui, speed, sf, ai, n8n) only when most of the change is its area, in the same message and reading the same pack. No check pass: the verdict is FAIL when `review.md` or any `review-<x>.md` has a MUST | `review.md`, `safety.md` `## After build` | Verdict PASS: `stage <id> review done` and `next <id> "/test <id>"` (`/test retest <id>` after fix-list items); `develop` and `fix` end here. A MUST: list each (`path:line` + fix), `stage <id> review fail`, back to Build, re-run only what failed; a re-review runs `review_pack.py <id>` again (a delta pack: the files changed since the review and the previous MUSTs) for the same reviewer |
| 5 | Test | The `test` skill (`/test <id>`, the same code path for every ticket): entry check, `qa-lead` plan (reused when the code is unchanged), the QA experts `test-plan.md` names in parallel, `qa-lead` (`sign-off`) | `test.md`, `qa-ui.md`, `qa-e2e.md`, `verify.md`, `qa-signoff.md`, `fix-list.md` | `qa-signoff.md` SIGNED OFF (`stage <id> test done` refuses otherwise). FAILED: `fix-list.md`, `next <id> "/fix <id>"`; fixes start only on the developer's word (the `fix` skill), then `/test retest <id>`. "Test later": the task waits at Test (the stage stays open) and cannot wrap until SIGNED OFF, or the developer accepts the risk in words (`qa-signoff.md` `## Verdict` as `SIGNED OFF - not tested, risk accepted by the developer: "<their words>" (<date>)`) |
| 6 | Verify | The `test` skill after SIGNED OFF (you): the criteria and smoke items the QA files did not cover (`live-check` when the app is up, otherwise against the diff); `verifier` when on the team; smoke items at risk medium or high | PASS/FAIL per criterion and smoke item in `status.md` | Every criterion and smoke item PASS. A restart needed first: say why and leave the stage open |
| 7 | Release (when on the team) | `release-manager`, after Verify | `release.md` | READY |
| 8 | Wrap-up | The `test` skill after SIGNED OFF: offer the `commit-msg` skill; `dev-handoff` when another owner must act; the lessons (section 9) | Commit message; `handoff-<developer>.md` | The developer has what they need to commit or hand over |
